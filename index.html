About

I'm a fan of WW2 films and have been building a Unity game set in that period, making many of its assets myself.

The first pack, WW2 Aircraft Mega Pack, grew out of that game's aircraft. Each one was finished with two paint schemes, three LODs, moving parts and effects, so they can be used in other developers' projects.
