How we build

Every model goes through the same checks before it ships.

True to the original

Shapes, proportions and markings follow the real aircraft and vehicles.

Ready for real time

Sensible triangle counts with LOD levels, sized 1 unit = 1 meter.

Moving parts

Parts that move in real life are separate, pivoted and animated.

Close-up inspected

Each model is checked from every angle for gaps, overlaps and see-through faces.

About

Iron Anvil Studio is an independent studio from South Korea making 3D game assets for Unity developers.

We started by building the aircraft for our own WW2 air combat game, then rebuilt every one of them to Asset Store quality so other developers can use them too.
