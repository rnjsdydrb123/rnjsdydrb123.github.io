WW2 Aircraft Mega Pack

Aircraft models with their moving parts and visual effects, from biplanes to four-engine bombers, including a jet fighter, a rocket glider and a flying bomb.

Moving parts

  • Retracting landing gear and gear doors
  • Bomb-bay doors and dive brakes
  • Ailerons, elevators and rudders
  • Propellers that blur into a disc at speed
  • Gun turrets with limited arcs

Parts sit on their own pivots and are moved by a script, not by animation clips. Canopies are fixed and do not open.

Also in the package

  • Gun effects: muzzle flashes and tracers at each gun's rate of fire
  • 21 particle effects: explosions, flak, fire, smoke, hits, torpedo wake and more
  • 10 ordnance prefabs: bombs, a torpedo, a rocket and drop tanks
  • Showroom demo scene in 12 languages
  • PDF documentation

Requirements

  • Unity 6.0 or newer
  • Universal Render Pipeline (URP) and Built-in Render Pipeline
  • HDRP is not supported
  • Real-world scale, 1 unit = 1 m

Not included: flight model, AI, damage system, terrain, water, buildings, ships and sound. You connect the aircraft to your own game code.
