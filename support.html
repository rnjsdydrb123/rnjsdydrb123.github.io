FAQ

Common questions about WW2 Aircraft Mega Pack.

Which Unity versions and render pipelines are supported?

Unity 6.0 or newer, with the Universal Render Pipeline (URP) or the Built-in Render Pipeline. HDRP is not supported.

Can the aircraft fly on their own?

No. The pack has models, moving parts and effects, but no flight model, AI or damage system. Your own flight code sends pitch, yaw, roll and throttle, and the control surfaces, propellers and gear follow.

Do the canopies open?

No. Canopies are fixed on every aircraft.

Can I use the assets in a commercial game?

Yes. Assets bought on the Unity Asset Store can be used in commercial projects under the standard Asset Store EULA.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by the Unity Asset Store, so refunds follow the Asset Store refund policy. You can email me first and I'll help where I can.
