FAQ

Answers to common questions.

Which Unity versions are supported?

Each asset lists its supported Unity versions and render pipelines on its Asset Store page.

Can I use the assets in a commercial game?

Yes. Assets bought on the Unity Asset Store can be used in your commercial projects under the standard Asset Store EULA.

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by the Unity Asset Store, so refund requests follow Unity's refund policy. Email us first and we'll help however we can.

Do you take custom requests?

Send us the details by email and we'll let you know if it's something we can do.
